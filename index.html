Résumé
Jarvis Resume November (PDF)

Jarvis is a software engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area. His work spans Python and Java backends, database-backed services, cloud applications, and cybersecurity research.

Jarvis earned a master’s degree in computer science from the University of Florida after completing a bachelor’s degree in computer science engineering at Bennett University. His portfolio includes a meal planner, an MRI classification research rebuild, and a series on backend engineering that uses a fictional bookstore to explain how APIs, databases, and background jobs fit together.

Early life and education

Jarvis was born and raised in Hyderabad. He moved to the Delhi region for undergraduate study at Bennett University in Greater Noida, where he earned a BTech in Computer Science Engineering, ranked in the top 2% of his class, and led the HackerEarth chapter. He also headed R&D for the AI Society and design for the Robotics Club.

At the University of Florida in Gainesville, he earned an MS in Computer Science with a 3.73 GPA. His coursework included natural language processing, advanced data structures, and analysis of algorithms.

Experience

University of Florida

As a lead graduate assistant in the Dean of Students Office from August 2023 to June 2024, he helped bring inventory records into PantrySOFT and worked with colleagues to forecast supply needs. The work combined software rollout with the daily operations of the Dean of Students Office.

Georgia Institute of Technology

From August to December 2022, he worked on Python anomaly detection and the CYBORG cybersecurity project, which studied how to detect compromised hosts in cyber-physical systems. The research looked at how software behavior and changes in a physical process can help distinguish a genuine anomaly from a false alarm.

Defence Research and Development Organisation

During a software engineering internship in 2022, he used Python and polynomial regression to model radome behavior for passive seeker systems.

Selected projects

  • LaiD — Meal planning and grocery checklists, rebuilt with React and Flask from an undergraduate project.
  • Big Brain MRI — Original research figures and a later TensorFlow and PyTorch rebuild verified on synthetic data.
  • Job Tracker — A full-stack application for tracking job applications.

Writing

Backend Engineering from First Principles follows a fictional bookstore from its first HTTP request through the design choices that come with running a service.

Personal interests

Outside work, Jarvis cycles, runs, follows Formula 1, and visits national parks. His longest ride was a 125.52-mile crossing of Florida. He is in ground school and aims to earn his private pilot license in 2027.
